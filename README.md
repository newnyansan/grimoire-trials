# Grimoire Trials

A 2D pixel-art magic platformer that runs in the browser.

- Take a personality trial to get one of thousands of magic types (30 elements × 7 rarity variants, randomized spells)
- Run through waves of enemies that grow ~18% stronger each wave, with a boss every 5th wave
- Mana, cooldowns, XP and levels
- **Spell Forge**: design your own spell — every spell must have a drawback
- Enemies drop items you can use as forge catalysts

## Play

**▶ [Play it live in your browser](https://newnyansan.github.io/grimoire-trials/)**

Or download and open `index.html` locally.
No install needed. Progress saves in your browser.

**Controls:** A/D move · W/Space jump · Q E R F skills · Left click/J basic attack · Shift dash · hold Right click/K to overcharge · Tab swap build · E use buildings in town · P/Esc pause · M mute
