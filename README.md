# Grimoire Trials

A 3D open-world magic game that runs in the browser.

**▶ [Play it live](https://newnyansan.github.io/grimoire-trials/)** (desktop, mouse + keyboard)

- Take the Trial at the shrine to receive one of thousands of grimoires
- Explore five regions, survive endless waves, clear Forgotten Crypts, rescue stranded villagers
- Grow camps into villages, towns and cities; defend them from raids
- Follow the quest chain to recover four Seal Shards and defeat Archmage Veyl
- Collect grimoire page fragments and bind them into your own spells; forge, upgrade and build loadouts

**Controls:** WASD move · Space jump · Shift dash · Q, E, Left click, Right click = spells · hold R to overcharge · F to use buildings, campfires and crypts · melee and aiming are automatic

3D character models: [KayKit Adventurers & Skeletons](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers) by Kay Lousberg (CC0), thank you!
